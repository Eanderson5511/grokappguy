        How it works & phone limits

        Dual Tube is a free page that uses the official YouTube player. Nothing is sold, and nothing is uploaded to a server of ours — your links, volumes, and audiobook position stay in this browser.

        • Desktop and Android Chrome: both decks can play at once, and each volume slider is honored by the player.
        • iPhone and iPad (Safari, and many in-app browsers): the system typically allows only one unmuted video at a time, and it usually ignores per-player volume. Use the device volume, and mute the deck you don’t need. The sliders are still here; they just may not take effect.
        • Phone calls, switching apps, and locking the screen: phones pause all browser audio during a call, and they usually pause it when the screen locks or you leave the app. That is an OS / browser limit. Dual Tube cannot reliably keep YouTube playing with the screen off or through a call. It does remember that you were playing and will try to resume both decks when you come back (call ends, screen on, app in front again).
        • Best chance on mobile: turn on Keep screen on, leave Dual Tube visible, or add it to your home screen (Install / Add to Home Screen). Lock-screen and headset controls are requested through the Media Session API when the browser allows them.
        • Search: type what you want (like “Atomic Habits audiobook” or “rain sounds”) and press Search. Tap a result to play it in that deck, or use the “→” button to send it to the other deck. Search uses free public servers, so if it is ever busy, pasting a link still works.
        • Paste a normal YouTube or YouTube Music link, a youtu.be short link, a Short, a playlist (list=), or just an 11-character video ID.
        • Compact mode shrinks the pictures so you can focus on audio. The players stay on the page (they are not hidden with display: none), because YouTube may stop a fully hidden player.
